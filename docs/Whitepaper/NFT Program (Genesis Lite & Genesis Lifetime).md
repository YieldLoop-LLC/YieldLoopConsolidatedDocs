# 7. NFT Program (Genesis Lite & Genesis Lifetime)

## 7.1 Overview

The YieldLoop NFT Program provides optional participation benefits.

NFTs:

- Do not alter LOOP liability math.
- Do not influence redemption coverage.
- Do not affect epoch isolation.
- Do not grant access to redemption pools.

NFTs modify fee treatment and incentive participation only.

---

## 7.2 Genesis Lite

Price: 300 USDT  
Supply: 3,300 total  
- 3,000 public  
- 300 reserved for marketing and partnerships  

Duration: 12 months from mint date  

Benefits:

- Performance fee reduction (applied to 10% base fee)
- Participation in Deposit Credit Pool
- Early feature access (if applicable)
- Community governance access (non-financial parameters only)

Genesis Lite benefits expire after 12 months unless renewed (if renewal mechanism is later introduced).

---

## 7.3 Genesis Lifetime

Price: 1,200 USDT  
Supply: 550 total  
- 500 public  
- 50 reserved for marketing and partnerships  

Duration: Lifetime  

Benefits:

- Larger performance fee reduction
- Larger share of Deposit Credit Pool
- Governance voting weight (non-liability affecting parameters)
- Early feature access
- Priority community participation access

Genesis Lifetime NFTs provide permanent incentive alignment without impacting system liability structure.

---

## 7.4 Performance Fee Adjustments

Base performance fee: 10%

NFT holders receive fee reduction as defined by governance parameters.

Important:

Fee reductions do not alter:

- Ratchet allocation percentage.
- Epoch isolation.
- Redemption coverage invariants.

Fee reductions apply only to user profit share calculation.

---

## 7.5 Deposit Credit Pool

A portion of marketing or treasury allocation may be directed toward a Deposit Credit Pool.

Deposit credits:

- May offset required vault minimums.
- May reward NFT holders proportionally.
- Do not mint LOOP.
- Do not alter redemption pools.
- Do not create liability.

Credits are promotional incentives only.

---

## 7.6 Governance Scope

NFT governance may include:

- Parameter voting (within predefined bounds)
- Risk cap adjustments
- UI feature decisions
- Future module approvals

NFT governance may not:

- Override coverage invariants
- Modify redemption formulas
- Alter epoch isolation
- Mint LOOP outside profit realization

Financial safety remains invariant-bound.

---

## 7.7 Design Philosophy

The NFT program exists to:

- Reward early supporters
- Align long-term participation
- Strengthen community governance

It does not exist to:

- Inflate yield
- Influence liability math
- Subsidize redemption

The financial engine remains independent of NFT supply or behavior.
